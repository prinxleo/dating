Android & Mobile Casting Guide

3 Ways to Cast Seamlessly on Android & iOS

1. Web & Video Co-Browse (Works on ALL Android Browsers)

Mobile Chrome restricts OS-level `getDisplayMedia()`. Use our built-in Web & Video Cast tab to watch MP4s, streams, websites, or beam instant screenshots in real-time between two Android phones!

2. Live Back/Front Camera & Audio Cast (Native WebRTC)

Tap Live Cam Cast to stream your front or rear camera + microphone directly to your partner via P2P WebRTC. You can flip between front & back cameras anytime.

3. Full OS Screen Mirroring (`getDisplayMedia`)

Works out-of-the-box on Desktop/Laptop Chrome, Edge, Safari, or inside custom Android WebView wrappers with media projection permissions enabled.
